## Design Context

### Users
The site serves a mixed audience: prospective students, academic researchers, collaborators, conference visitors, and culturally engaged members of the public. The homepage should orient newcomers quickly while also signaling serious scholarship and active field research.

### Brand Personality
Scholarly, archaeological, contemporary, prestigious, and modern. The interface should feel like a premium research institute rather than a generic university microsite: confident, cultured, and grounded in material evidence, fieldwork, and archival depth.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a dark theme with premium greens, blues, and teals. The visual language should feel editorial and museum-grade, with a sense of excavation, rainforest depth, and historical gravity. It should avoid generic corporate cards, dashboard tropes, and templated "academic" layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with institutional authority and research substance, not filler copy.
2. Balance archaeology and contemporary scholarship so site feels both historical and current.
3. Use visual composition, typography, and texture to create prestige without looking ornamental or old-fashioned.
4. Surface the institute's strongest signals early: founding context, PARU Uaxactun, technologies, collections, publications, and student pathways.
5. Keep motion subtle and purposeful, with strong readability and responsive behavior across desktop and mobile.
