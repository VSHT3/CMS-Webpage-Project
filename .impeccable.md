## Design Context

### Users
The site serves students, teachers, public visitors, academics, and researchers interested in Mesoamerican studies at Comenius University. Visitors use it to understand the center, find research activity, browse publications and collections, learn about education paths, and locate institutional contact information.

### Brand Personality
Clean, working, academic, honest. The voice should feel direct, serious, legible, and institutionally grounded, with enough visual character to fit Mesoamerican studies without becoming theatrical, promotional, or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
The interface should read as a clean university faculty website: restrained, credible, content-led, and navigable. Visual character should come from real subject matter such as fieldwork, artifacts, collections, publications, maps, and institutional details, not from generic dark gradients, glass effects, neon accents, or repeated promotional cards.

### Design Principles
- Academic clarity first: navigation, hierarchy, and content legibility matter more than spectacle.
- Let the subject carry the character: use artifacts, fieldwork, collections, publications, and institutional details instead of generic decoration.
- Respect the university context: polished and credible, but not corporate SaaS, luxury editorial, or museum fantasy.
- Keep performance visible in the experience: pages should feel light, stable, and dependable on student and public visitor devices.
- Avoid AI slop: every visual pattern should have a reason connected to the center, the faculty, or the content.
