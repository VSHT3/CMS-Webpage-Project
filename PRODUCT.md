# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves students, teachers, public visitors, academics, and researchers interested in Mesoamerican studies at Comenius University. Visitors use it to understand the faculty center, find research activity, browse publications and collections, learn about education paths, and locate institutional contact information.

## Product Purpose

The site presents the Center for Mesoamerican Studies as a credible academic institution with active research, teaching, fieldwork, collections, publications, and public-facing scholarship. Success means visitors can quickly understand what the center does, trust its academic seriousness, and navigate to the right institutional section without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clean, working, academic, honest. The voice should feel direct, serious, legible, and institutionally grounded, with enough visual character to fit Mesoamerican studies without becoming theatrical or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated landing page aesthetics, dark neon tech palettes, decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, repeated card grids, empty hero metrics, and anything that feels outside a clean university faculty website. Avoid museum-fantasy styling that makes the center look fictional or promotional rather than academic.

## Design Principles

- Academic clarity first: navigation, hierarchy, and content legibility matter more than spectacle.
- Let the subject carry the character: use artifacts, fieldwork, collections, publications, and institutional details instead of generic decoration.
- Respect the university context: polished and credible, but not corporate SaaS or luxury editorial.
- Keep performance visible in the experience: pages should feel light, stable, and dependable on student and public visitor devices.
- Avoid AI slop: every visual pattern should have a reason connected to the center, the faculty, or the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as the baseline. Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, meaningful alt text for research and collection imagery, descriptive iframe titles, reduced motion preferences, and responsive layouts for mobile visitors.
